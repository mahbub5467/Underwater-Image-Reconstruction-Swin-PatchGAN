# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in this research repository.

## Before contributing
- Open an issue to discuss substantial changes.
- Keep changes focused and document the motivation.
- Do not upload EUVP images or trained weights unless redistribution rights are clear.
- Do not claim a reproduction result unless the exact data split, code, configuration, and evaluation protocol are recorded.

## Pull requests
Please include:
- A concise description of the change.
- Environment and command used to test it.
- Relevant logs or metric summaries for model/evaluation changes.
- Any change to dataset preprocessing or evaluation protocol.

## Research integrity
Preserve reported paper results as published. Clearly label newly reproduced or extended experiments and do not overwrite the paper's reported tables with new values.
